 #Anime-Universe-Dashboard


1 - Project Title / Headline:

🎌 Anime Universe: Ratings, Genres & Popularity Dashboard:-

An interactive and visually stunning Power BI dashboard built to explore anime data — focusing on genre popularity, ratings distribution, type analysis, popularity trends, and year-wise rating patterns from 1963 to 2024.


2 - Short Description / Purpose:

The Anime Universe Dashboard is a visually themed and analytically powerful Power BI report designed to help users explore and understand the anime industry through data. Built on a dataset of 18,600+ anime titles across 21 unique genres and 6 types — this dashboard highlights key metrics like total anime count, average rating, highest popularity, total genres, and year range. This tool is built for anime enthusiasts, data analysts, content researchers, and Power BI learners who want to understand what genres are trending, which anime are most popular, and how ratings have evolved over the decades.


3 - Tech Stack:

* 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for building all visuals and reports
* 🗂️ Power Query – Used for cleaning and transforming the raw anime dataset
* 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPI cards, calculated columns like Rating Bin and Genre Single, and custom measures like    Date Range and Total Genres
* 📅 Data Modeling – Filters and slicers set up for dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals
* 🐍 Python (Pandas) – Used for initial data cleaning and removing duplicates and null values
* 📁 File Format – .pbit for Power BI report template
* 🎨 Theme – Attack on Titan inspired dark theme for immersive visual experience


4 - Data Source:

Source: Kaggle --  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nalisha/anime-dataset-ratings-genres-and-popularity

Dataset contains anime records from 1963 to 2024 — covering ratings, popularity, genres, episode counts, studios, and type information for 18,600+ unique anime titles.

Key Fields:

* Title – Name of the anime
* Type – Format of the anime (TV / Movie / OVA / Special / ONA / Music)
* Score – Rating out of 10 given by users
* Genres – Genre categories (Action / Comedy / Drama / Fantasy and more)
* Members – Number of users who have this anime in their list
* Year – Year the anime was released
* Studios – Production studio name
* Episodes – Total number of episodes


5 - Features / Highlights:

a- Business Problem:

The anime industry has grown massively over the past 60 years — with thousands of titles released across dozens of genres and formats. Without a structured analytical view, it becomes very difficult to understand which genres are most popular, how ratings have changed over time, and which anime have built the largest fan base. Casual viewers and researchers both need a clear and simple way to explore this massive dataset.

b- Goal of the Dashboard:

The goal of this Anime Universe Dashboard is to provide one single interactive report where anyone can:

* ✅ See total anime count, average rating, popularity, genres, and year range using KPI Cards
* ✅ Understand how ratings are distributed using Ratings Distribution Bar Chart
* ✅ Discover the most popular genres using Top 10 Genres Chart
* ✅ See anime format breakdown using Anime by Type Donut Chart
* ✅ Track how ratings improved over decades using Average Rating Over Years Line Chart
* ✅ Explore the most popular anime titles using Top 3 Most Popular Anime Table with Images
* ✅ Understand the relationship between popularity and rating using Scatter Plot
* ✅ Filter the entire dashboard by anime type using Interactive Slicer


c- Walk Through of Key Visuals:

* KPI Cards (Top Row): Show Total Anime, Average Rating, Highest Members Count, Total Unique Genres, and Year Range — gives an instant snapshot of the entire dataset in one glance

* Ratings Distribution (Bar Chart): Shows how anime titles are spread across rating ranges from 1-2 to 9-10 — clearly showing that most anime fall in the 5-6 and 6-7 rating range

* Top 10 Genres by Count (Horizontal Bar Chart): Ranks all 21 genres by total anime count — Action leads as the most common genre followed by Adventure and Comedy

* Anime by Type (Donut Chart): Breaks down all anime across 6 formats — TV dominates with 60%+ of all titles followed by Movies and OVA

* Average Rating Over the Years (Line Chart): Shows how average ratings have improved from 1963 to 2024 — clear upward trend showing that anime quality has improved over time

* Top 3 Most Popular Anime (Table with Images): Displays the top 3 most popular anime by member count with poster images — Shingeki no Kyojin, Death Note, and Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

* Popularity vs Rating (Scatter Plot): Visualizes the relationship between popularity and rating for all anime — showing a weak positive correlation of 0.35, meaning popular anime are not always the highest rated

* Interactive Slicer (Filter by Type): Allows users to filter the entire dashboard by anime type — TV, Movie, OVA, Special, ONA, or Music — with just one click


d- Business Impact & Insights:

* 🏆 Action is the most produced genre — leading with 2,287 anime titles
* 📺 TV format dominates the anime industry — contributing 60%+ of all titles
* 📈 Average ratings have steadily improved — from 6.2 in the 1970s to 7.1 in 2020
* 🔥 Shingeki no Kyojin (Attack on Titan) leads in popularity — with 4.3M+ members
* ⭐ Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood is the highest rated anime — with a rating of 9.19 out of 10
* 📊 Popularity and Rating have only 0.35 correlation — proving that the most popular anime are not always the best rated
* 🎌 Dataset spans over 60 years — from 1963 to 2024 covering the full history of modern anime
